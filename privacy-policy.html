Privacy Policy

This policy explains how POCKFUME handles information customers choose to provide when enquiring about or placing an order.

Information we may receive

Depending on your order or enquiry, this may include your name, WhatsApp/phone number, delivery address, order details, and messages you send us.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and customer-support requests.
  • To confirm and process orders and arrange delivery.
  • To communicate order status or resolve order issues.
  • To send information about offers and new fragrances only where appropriate consent has been obtained.
  • To meet legal obligations and protect against misuse or fraud.

Sharing

We may share only the information reasonably needed with service providers involved in fulfilling an order, such as delivery/courier services, or where required by law. We do not state that information is never shared.

Promotional messages and choices

You may ask us to stop promotional messages at any time through WhatsApp. Order-related or service messages may still be sent when necessary to fulfil an order or respond to your request.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to handle information carefully and retain it only as needed for the purposes described above, legal requirements, and resolving disputes. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Requests and questions

For privacy questions or requests concerning your information, contact POCKFUME on WhatsApp at +91 96637 12870.
