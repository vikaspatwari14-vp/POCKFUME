The pocket perfume guide

Solid perfume for everyday life.

POCKFUME makes compact solid fragrance for people who want an easy-to-carry scent. Explore pocket perfume for college, daily routines and travel, then choose the fragrance that fits your mood.

What is solid perfume?

Solid perfume is fragrance in a compact, balm-like format rather than a liquid spray. POCKFUME comes in a pocket-sized 10 g format that you can apply with a fingertip.

Is pocket perfume convenient for college?

A pocket perfume is easy to carry in a bag or pouch for a quick fragrance touch-up between classes, after a commute or before meeting friends.

Can I carry solid perfume while travelling?

POCKFUME's compact solid format is designed for everyday carry and travel. Keep the tin closed when it is in your bag and follow any transport or airline rules that apply to your journey.

How do I choose a POCKFUME fragrance?

Explore the collection for different fragrance moods, including fresh, spicy, woody, warm and floral styles. Read each scent description and choose the one that best suits your preference.
